Gameplay

Built for real rides

8 Courses (+ Reverse)

Penguin Pass (built-in 5 km alpine loop), Rolling 5 / 10 / 20 km, Mountain 10 km, Desert 10 / 20 km, and Flat 10 km — rerollable seeds, dynamic weather and time of day, and reverse direction with separate personal bests.

15 Built-In Workouts + .zwo

Recovery through VO₂max and an FTP ramp test with ERG resistance control and live ±25% difficulty bias. Import Zwift .zwo files or pick from bundled structured sessions.

Real Smart-Trainer Physics

Grade simulation, aerodynamic drafting, pacing bots, timed sprint and climb segments, and personal-best ghosts on every lap.

Zwift Controllers & Steam Deck

Native BLE for Zwift Play, Click, and Ride — steering, menus, powerups, and 24-speed virtual shifting. Full Steam Deck gamepad and keyboard layouts.

Offline .fit + Optional Strava

Every ride saves locally as a standard .fit activity file. Connect Strava only when you want one-click uploads with your own API keys.

Built-In TV Dashboard

Live telemetry and an elevation chart in any phone, tablet, or TV browser on your LAN — zero companion app required.
